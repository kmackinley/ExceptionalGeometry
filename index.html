Open problems

The shortest path to a decisive result

The next gains should come from closing operator, topology, and normalization gaps—not adding more numerical coincidences.

Define one fundamental action

Resolve Einstein–Yang–Mills versus pure Yang–Mills emergent gravity and state the independent scales.

Prove the equivariant Dirac index

Establish physical multiplicity and chirality, not merely fixed-point counts.

Repair the instanton sector

Use valid twists, bundle topology, higher charge, or a genuine global Spin(7) solution.

Compute the Higgs potential

Derive or reject λ=4/31 from the Wilson-line Coleman–Weinberg potential.

Derive flavor overlaps

Normalize modes and calculate CKM/PMNS quantities directly.

Continue beyond the brane obstruction

Search exceptional modes, full backreaction, additional branes, and disconnected branches.
