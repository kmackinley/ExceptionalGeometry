# Research Status

**Last reviewed:** 17 August 2026

This page classifies the current manuscripts by the strongest status supported by their contents. “Theorem” here means a proof inside the explicitly defined mathematical model; it is not a statement of external peer-review acceptance or physical validation.

## Interpretation policy

The Coxeter element and associated torus are treated as mathematical structures. Fixed-point and determinant statements are not, by themselves, statements that particles occupy literal positions in an internal torus or that the universe is a Coxeter torus. Any mapping to generations, masses, couplings, or spacetime is a separate model hypothesis and is classified accordingly. See [MODEL_SCOPE.md](MODEL_SCOPE.md).

## Dependency rule

A public result cannot be assigned a stronger status than its weakest indispensable premise. For example, an exact numerical consequence of a conjectured Higgs potential remains a **conjecture**, not a theorem.

## Model variants that must remain separate

### Variant A — Einstein–Yang–Mills starting point

The synthesis manuscript includes a twelve-dimensional Einstein–Hilbert term, Yang–Mills field, and adjoint fermion.

### Variant B — Pure Yang–Mills emergent gravity

The Newton manuscript describes pure twelve-dimensional \(E_8\) Yang–Mills as the complete starting action and identifies the frame field in mixed-index gauge components.

Until a single action and reduction are specified consistently, the repository treats these as **two variants under investigation**.
